Clinic Connect

A school healthcare services system that connects students, teachers, and clinic staff for convenient health requests, referrals, and appointment management.

How Clinic Connect works
Student requests a checkup

Submit a clinic appointment request and provide your health concern and preferred schedule.

Teacher refers a student

Teachers can send referrals to the clinic when a student needs medical attention.

Clinic staff manages requests

Clinic staff review requests, manage appointments, and maintain student health records.
